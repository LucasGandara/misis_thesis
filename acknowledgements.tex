\chapter{Acknowledgements}
My undergraduate project was about the application of DQN algorithm in turtlebot3 robot to avoid dynamic obstacle in a virtual environment imitating the laboratory of the Universidad Militar Nueva Granada. There , I applied the DQN algorithm on top of an $A^*$ algorithm that would take control when the path planing would cause a crash due to the dynamic obstacles. The project was supervised by Dr. Nelson Velasco and Dr. Alexandra Velasco, who provided me with valuable insights and support throughout the project. Their expertise in the field of robotics and reinforcement learning has been instrumental in shaping my understanding of the subject matter. I saw the potential of RL and I wanted to explore more algorithms, I ended up studying in deep all its form, from vanilla policy gradient to TRPO and PPO. And from tabular Q-learning to TD3 and SAC, but all in simple environments like inverted pendulum. I wanted to apply all this knowledge in a more complex environment. I was not sure to start a master degree.
My father encouraged me to apply to the Universidad de los Andes, and I was accepted, he was right, it was the best decision I could have made.

I would like to thank my advisor, Dr. Nicolas Cardozo, for his guidance and support throughout this project. His expertise and insights have been invaluable in shaping my understanding of the subject matter.
